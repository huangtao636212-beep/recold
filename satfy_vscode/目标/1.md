    DEVA->>FCLS: Diagnostic result(s)

    FCLS->>SDEC: Fault level + latch state
    SDEC->>REAC: Determine reaction (trip/stop/degrade)
    REAC->>ENF: Enforce safe state (if required)
    ENF->>OUT: De-energize / disable outputs

    Note over EXT,OUT: Total reaction time <= 80 ms (worst case)
